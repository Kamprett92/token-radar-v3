    Methodology — radar-3

    Implied value = TVL × category × growth × age × breadth × leadership × velocity. Each factor is computed from the snapshot at run time; nothing is hardcoded by name.

    • Category: the trimmed multiple of the category's live-token market-cap/TVL ratios — ratios clipped to their interquartile range, then averaged (median for 3–4 comparables; the global trimmed mean, 0.382, when fewer than 3). Clamped to 0.05–1.50. A raw median ignores how wildly comparables disagree (Lending's ratios run 0.008 to 18.956); the trimmed mean uses the whole distribution.
    • Growth: 1 + 0.5 × the protocol's 7-day TVL change relative to the market median (−8.23% this snapshot), clamped to −50%/+100%. A protocol growing while the market falls scores for it.
    • Age: 0.80 + 0.20 × min(age, 730 days)/730 — older with no token scores higher; unknown age scores 0.90.
    • Breadth: 1 + 0.08 × ln(chains)/ln(32), capped at 1.08 — running on more chains is wider distribution.
    • Leadership: 1.12 for the largest pre-token protocol in its category by TVL, 1.06 for 2nd–3rd, else 1.00.
    • Velocity: TVL per day since listing, in quartiles across the board — top quartile 1.08, third 1.03, second 1.00, bottom 0.95. Fast accumulation is a hot launch; slow accumulation is not.

    Board: every protocol that records no token and is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product, a versioned copy or an exchange product — 201 rows, the 40-protocol watchlist included. Farm plans are 7 steps each: capital by TVL tier ($250–$5,000), hold window by age (90/180/365 days), chain-aware, with an exit plan and a risk note where the data supports one. Implied values are this entry's own claim, never make.fun's; "records no token" means DefiLlama's record carries none, not that none exists.
